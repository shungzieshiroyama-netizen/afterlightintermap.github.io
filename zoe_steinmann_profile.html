VI Supervisor Assessment

Caracal maintains exceptional combat efficiency despite observable psychological irregularities. Engagements are executed with precision, speed, and calculated lethality, with no degradation in judgement or situational awareness. Her trauma response appears to heighten operational output, resulting in sharper reaction times and more decisive eliminations under pressure. Movements remain controlled, with aggression applied deliberately rather than erratically. From a tactical standpoint, she is highly reliable and remains a top-tier asset; however, the dependence on trauma as a performance catalyst introduces a latent risk factor should her condition destabilise beyond current control.

Supervisory Assessment — Synthetic Unit · Shuzika Ruzinaki

She carries herself like a child who has learned far too early to be quiet. When left alone, she grows still in a way that feels wrong. The small twitches worry me… they are brief, but they feel like something inside her is struggling to settle. She does not cry, does not speak of what she has seen… and that silence weighs heavier than anything she could say. Yet when she is placed into danger, she becomes composed… too composed… moving with a certainty that no child should possess. It is as though she has taken all that hurt and folded it neatly inside herself, using it to keep going. She is not healing, not truly… she is enduring. And I cannot help but feel she should not have to do so alone.

Welfare Observation · Helena Crove
