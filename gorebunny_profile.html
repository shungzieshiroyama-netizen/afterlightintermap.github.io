Before Log 01

Chronoshii Kate "Gorebunny" Ruzinaki was once an ordinary college student. Four years ago, her greatest concerns were lectures, assignments, examinations, and whatever mundane problems occupied the mind of a young woman who still believed there would be a tomorrow.

Then the world began to scream.

Kate was among the scattered few who survived the Howling Plague, though the person who entered the apocalypse was not quite the same person who remained four years later. The collapse of civilisation forced her to abandon university, her home, and nearly every ordinary expectation she once had for her future. What began as an unwilling struggle for survival gradually became a way of life.

Despite everything she has endured, Kate remains remarkably cheerful.

She is quirky, playful, and almost absurdly energetic. She smiles constantly, cracks jokes when she probably shouldn't, and possesses an almost irritating ability to find amusement in otherwise miserable circumstances. A ruined supermarket can become an exciting scavenging expedition. An abandoned military checkpoint becomes a treasure trove of equipment. Finding an intact box of ammunition can elicit more excitement from her than finding something genuinely valuable.

Her old classmates might have recognised the same girl beneath all of it.

Just considerably more armed.

Marksmanship & Ammunition Discipline Log 02

Kate developed a fascination with firearms before the collapse, but the apocalypse transformed that interest into genuine expertise. Four years of necessity taught her how to maintain weapons, conserve ammunition, judge distances, identify useful equipment, and most importantly, shoot without wasting a round.

She understands that ammunition cannot simply be replaced anymore. Every cartridge represents something that may not be available again.

Discipline · every round accounted for

Engagement Doctrine — Howlers Log 03

As a Marksman, Kate prefers to keep her distance from Howlers. She is an excellent observer and can spend considerable time watching a location before deciding whether it is safe. She studies Howler movement, listens for their calls, identifies pack behaviour, and uses terrain to her advantage.

Yet Kate's greatest advantage may be that she doesn't behave like someone who is afraid of them.

She behaves like someone who has spent four years learning them.

⚠ Threat Note — Howlers Still terrifying creatures. Their speed, pack behaviour, and predatory instincts make close encounters extraordinarily dangerous. Kate knows this better than most. She has seen what happens when someone panics, wastes ammunition, or allows themselves to become surrounded.

Consequently, when combat begins, her playful demeanour can vanish beneath something considerably colder.

The grin remains.

Her hands simply stop shaking.

Kate is brutal when fighting Howlers. She does not romanticise killing them, nor does she particularly enjoy the suffering involved. To her, a Howler is a former human being whose mind and body have been catastrophically altered. That knowledge makes killing them uncomfortable, but survival leaves little room for sentimentality when a pack is closing in.

She aims to end encounters quickly.

Kate is also willing to abandon conventional ideas of honour when necessary. She will retreat, hide, misdirect a pack, exploit terrain, or simply wait for a better opportunity. She has no interest in dying heroically.

The world already has enough graves.

Fieldcraft & Rosebridge Log 04

Her survival skills extend beyond marksmanship. Kate has learned to scavenge abandoned buildings, identify edible plants, preserve food, ration supplies, navigate without reliable infrastructure, establish temporary shelters, and recognise signs of nearby Howler activity. She is particularly careful with resources because she remembers a time when wasting something was merely inconvenient.

Now, wasting something can get someone killed.

The camp outside Rosebridge has become one of the few places Kate considers home. It lacks reliable electricity, relying on stockpiled fuel and batteries, and food has become increasingly scarce. Kate contributes primarily through reconnaissance, scavenging, hunting, and protecting the camp from Howler packs.

She is particularly fond of wandering farther from camp than anyone would prefer.

Not because she is reckless.

At least, that is what she insists.

What Remains Log 05

Kate's greatest contradiction is that she still possesses the personality of the girl she used to be. She laughs. She teases. She gets excited over little things. She occasionally talks about university as though she might return to it someday.

She knows she probably won't.

Still, she refuses to let the apocalypse take everything from her.

The world may have ended.

Kate simply decided that she hadn't.

On the Name Log 10

The nickname "Gorebunny" is an affectionate contradiction. Kate looks almost comically cheerful for someone who spends her days carrying a rifle through the ruins of civilisation. Her bright smile, playful personality, and almost bunny-like enthusiasm make the name appropriate.

The "Gore" part came later.

People who have watched her work know why.
